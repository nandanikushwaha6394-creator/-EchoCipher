Known Limitations

  • Heavily compressed telephony audio (below 8 kbps) reduces accuracy by roughly 6 points.
  • Clips shorter than 1.5 seconds often fall close to the decision threshold.
  • Overlapping speakers are not separated; the dominant voice drives the verdict.
  • Languages outside the training set are supported but under-validated.
